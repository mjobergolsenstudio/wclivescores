The Favourites

🥇

France 🇫🇷 — Tournament Favourite

Mbappé, Griezmann, Dembélé and the best squad depth in the world. France are built for the knockout format — they can grind out 1-0 wins or destroy teams 4-0. The team to beat.

🥈

England 🏴󠁧󠁢󠁥󠁮󠁧󠁿 — Finally Their Time?

Bellingham, Kane, Saka, Foden — arguably England's greatest ever generation. The question is always mental strength in the big moments. They have the squad to win it.

🥉

Brazil 🇧🇷 — Always Dangerous

Vinícius Jr leads a squad that blends flair with organisation. Brazil haven't won since 2002 and the hunger is real. Expect them to go deep.

4️⃣

Germany 🇩🇪 — Resurgent

Musiala, Wirtz and Havertz form one of the most exciting attacks in Europe. Germany are rebuilding but this team has the quality to go all the way. Dark horse for the title.

5️⃣

Argentina 🇦🇷 — Defending Champions

Messi plays his last World Cup at 38, still dangerous, still orchestrating. The rest of the squad is strong. Expect a Quarter Final exit — the 2022 magic is hard to replicate.

Dark Horses 🌑

⚡

Spain 🇪🇸

Lamine Yamal at just 18 will be the story of the tournament. Spain's possession football is relentless and suffocating. They could absolutely win this.

⚡

Portugal 🇵🇹

Bernardo Silva, Rúben Neves, Vitinha, Rafael Leão — with or without Ronaldo leading, Portugal have the quality to win the tournament.

⚡

Netherlands 🇳🇱

Van Dijk at the back, Gakpo and Depay going forward. They peaked at the 2022 Quarter Finals — this time they go further.

🇳🇴

Norway — The Wildcard

Erling Haaland set a UEFA qualifying record with 16 goals. If Norway escape Group I, Haaland in a knockout game is terrifying. Norway could be the tournament's story.
