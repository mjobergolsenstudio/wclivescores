France
FIFA #2 · Group I · Tournament favourite
✅ Strong form

France arrive in excellent shape after beating Brazil 2-1 in a March friendly with the first team, and Colombia 3-1 with the B team — showing remarkable squad depth. Mbappé leads the attack at 27, in his prime. Dembélé, Griezmann and Tchouaméni give Deschamps options in every area.

Key concern: France have historically underperformed their squad quality at World Cups. Mental pressure is their only real enemy.

Squad named: May 26
Key player: Mbappé
Group: I
Opener: Jun 15 vs Senegal
🏴󠁧󠁢󠁥󠁮󠁧󠁿
England
FIFA #4 · Group L · Strong favourite
✅ Squad named May 22

Thomas Tuchel names his England squad on Friday May 22 — one of the most anticipated squad announcements of the tournament. England have Bellingham, Kane, Saka and Foden — arguably their greatest ever generation. Tuchel must forge a clear identity after Southgate's near-misses at Euro 2020 and 2024.

Key concern: England have historically struggled in heat and fatigue after a long Premier League season. Managing fitness across 7 potential matches is critical.

Squad: May 22
Key player: Bellingham
Group: L
Opener: Jun 17 vs Croatia
🇧🇷
Brazil
FIFA #5 · Group C · Always dangerous
⚠️ Injury concerns

Carlo Ancelotti confirmed his 26-man Brazil squad on May 18, with veteran Neymar making the cut. However, Brazil face significant injury concerns: Éder Militão, Rodrygo and Estevão are all confirmed out of the tournament. Alisson's fitness from a Liverpool injury is also uncertain.

Vinícius Jr and Raphinha lead the attack, supported by a strong midfield duo anchored by Casemiro. Brazil are building without a traditional striker — an interesting tactical experiment from Ancelotti.

Squad: May 18 ✅
Key player: Vinícius Jr
Group: C
Opener: Jun 12 vs Morocco
🇦🇷
Argentina
FIFA #1 · Group J · Defending champions
✅ Messi confirmed

Lionel Messi was named in Argentina's 55-man preliminary squad on May 11 — confirming he will appear in his sixth World Cup, breaking the all-time record. At 38, expectations are managed, but Messi's presence gives the entire squad an extra level of motivation.

Argentina's strength is mental fortitude and collective identity rather than individual brilliance. Álvarez and Lautaro Martínez lead the attack, with Enzo Fernández and Mac Allister controlling midfield. Final 26-man squad to be confirmed.

Squad: Final June 1
Key player: Messi
Group: J
Opener: Jun 16 vs Algeria
🇪🇸
Spain
FIFA #3 · Group H · Top-seeded team
✅ In excellent form

Spain enter as the top-seeded team in the entire tournament — drawn into the same bracket half as Argentina to prevent them meeting before the final. Lamine Yamal, just 18, is the story of European football and will be the tournament's most-watched player.

Spain's tiki-taka system has evolved into something more dynamic. Pedri, Gavi and Yamal give them the most exciting midfield-attack combination in the world. Morata leads the line.

Key player: Lamine Yamal
Group: H
Opener: Jun 15 vs Cape Verde
🇩🇪
Germany
FIFA #12 · Group E · Resurgent
✅ Squad announced May 21

Germany announce their final squad on May 21. After years of underperforming, this German side looks genuinely dangerous again. Florian Wirtz has emerged as one of Europe's most exciting players at Bayer Leverkusen, while Musiala and Havertz complete a devastating attacking trio.

Germany are in arguably the easiest group — Curaçao, Ivory Coast and Ecuador — giving them every chance of arriving in the knockout stage with momentum and confidence.

Squad: May 21
Key player: Wirtz
Group: E
Opener: Jun 13 vs Curaçao
🇵🇹
Portugal
FIFA #6 · Group K · Dark horse
✅ Ronaldo confirmed

Cristiano Ronaldo, 41, is expected to lead Portugal's attack — almost certainly his final World Cup. But this Portugal team is far more than Ronaldo. Bernardo Silva, Rafael Leão, Vitinha and Bruno Fernandes form one of the most technically gifted squads in the tournament.

Portugal face DR Congo, Uzbekistan and Colombia in Group K — a very winnable group that could see them arrive at the knockout stage as a dangerous, in-form team.

Key player: Bernardo Silva
Group: K
Opener: Jun 16 vs DR Congo
🇳🇱
Netherlands
FIFA #7 · Group F · Quarter final favourites
✅ Squad May 27

The Netherlands announce their final squad on May 27. Ronald Koeman has built a balanced side with Van Dijk commanding at the back, Gakpo dangerous in attack and De Jong controlling midfield. They reached the quarter finals in 2022 — expect them to go further this time.

Group F with Japan, Sweden and Tunisia is eminently winnable, and a potential knockout run against lower-seeded teams could take them deep.

Squad: May 27
Key player: Van Dijk
Group: F
Opener: Jun 14 vs Japan
🇧🇪
Belgium
FIFA #3 · Group G · Dark horse
✅ Squad confirmed

Belgium's squad is confirmed: Courtois in goal, De Bruyne in midfield, Lukaku leading the attack. The so-called "golden generation" has finally given way to a more balanced team — De Bruyne at 35 may play his last World Cup as the engine of a dangerous side.

Doku, De Ketelaere and Trossard provide pace and creativity in attack. Belgium in Group G face Egypt, Iran and New Zealand — they should qualify comfortably and could go deep in the knockout stage.

Key player: De Bruyne
Group: G
Opener: Jun 14 vs Egypt
🇳🇴
Norway
FIFA #27 · Group I · The wildcard
✅ Haaland confirmed

Norway are back at the World Cup after 28 years — and Erling Haaland set a UEFA qualifying record with 16 goals. Group I with France, Senegal and Iraq is tough but navigable. The Iraq match on June 15 is a must-win, and Haaland against that defence is a mismatch.

Ødegaard provides the creativity, Berge the defensive cover and Sörloth the physical backup. Norway are not here to make up numbers.

Key player: Haaland
Group: I
Opener: Jun 15 vs Iraq
