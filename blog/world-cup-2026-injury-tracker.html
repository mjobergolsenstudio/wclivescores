🔴 Confirmed Out — Will Not Play

🇧🇷
Estêvão OUT
Brazil · Winger · Chelsea

The 17-year-old prodigy suffered a hamstring injury against Manchester United in April that left him in tears on the pitch. Initial assessments were optimistic, but it soon became clear the damage was far more serious. Brazil's most exciting young talent will miss the entire tournament — one of the biggest heartbreaks of the summer.

🇧🇷
Alisson Becker OUT
Brazil · Goalkeeper · Liverpool

Brazil's first-choice goalkeeper announced on Instagram that he would be "out for a while." The 33-year-old has battled multiple injuries this season and was ruled out of Brazil's final friendlies before the tournament. Ederson (Man City) is now Brazil's number one.

🇳🇱
Matthijs de Ligt OUT
Netherlands · Centre-back · Manchester United

De Ligt has not played since November 30 following a persistent back issue that required additional surgery. Manchester United do not expect him back until early in the 2026-27 season. A huge blow for the Dutch backline.

🇯🇵
Xavi Simons OUT
Netherlands · Midfielder · RB Leipzig

Simons tore his ACL at Tottenham in April. He attempted a remarkable comeback, resuming training in March, but failed to convince the selectors and was not named in the Dutch squad. An extraordinary effort that ultimately fell short.

🟡 Racing to Be Fit — Doubtful

🇦🇷
Lionel Messi DOUBTFUL
Argentina · Forward · Inter Miami

Messi is training with Argentina but has been among a group of players working separately with the physiotherapy team. The Argentine FA confirmed he is "making good progress" and is expected to be available for the opening match on June 16 against Algeria. Several teammates — including Emiliano Martínez, Leandro Paredes, and Nahuel Molina — are in the same category. Cristian Romero and Julián Álvarez were cleared and available.

🇪🇸
Fermín López MONITORING
Spain · Midfielder · Barcelona

Spain's European champions are monitoring several injury concerns in their squad as the tournament approaches. Fermín López is among those being carefully managed ahead of June 15's opener against Cape Verde.

✅ Made It Back — Cleared to Play

🇫🇷
William Saliba CLEARED
France · Centre-back · Arsenal

Saliba was a concern after picking up a knock late in the Arsenal season, but is on track to play for France and expected to start in Group I against Senegal on June 15.

🇦🇷
Cristian Romero CLEARED
Argentina · Centre-back · Tottenham

Romero has been cleared and is available for Argentina's friendly against Honduras and the tournament opener on June 16.

The Bigger Picture

The expanded 48-team format and 104-match schedule means there are more players than ever at the World Cup — but the injury news heading in is significant. Brazil losing both their starting goalkeeper and their most exciting teenage talent before a ball is kicked is a major blow to their title chances.

For Argentina, managing Messi's fitness across a potential seven matches (if they reach the final) will be the central challenge for Scaloni. At 38, every minute counts.
