😮 1. Spain 0–0 Cape Verde — The Biggest Shock of the Group Stage

Group H · June 15Spain 🇪🇸 0 – 0 🇨🇻 Cape Verde
SHOCK

Spain were the reigning European champions. Cape Verde had never beaten a major European nation at a World Cup. The result was the single biggest surprise of the entire group stage — and the largest lost bet in 2026 World Cup history according to bookmakers.

At the centre of it was Cape Verde goalkeeper Vozinha, whose performance went instantly viral. Save after save. A man possessed. Spain hit the post, had three efforts cleared off the line, and still couldn't find a way through. Cape Verde celebrated the 0-0 like a trophy. They had earned every second of it.

Cape Verde went on to qualify from Group H as runners-up. They face Argentina in the Round of 32. Vozinha is already the tournament's cult hero.

🇲🇦 2. Morocco Hold Brazil to a Draw

Group C · June 13Brazil 🇧🇷 1 – 1 🇲🇦 Morocco
SURPRISE

Morocco are no longer the underdog they once were — they reached the semi-finals in 2022. But holding a Brazil side managed by Carlo Ancelotti to a draw in their opening match was still a significant statement. Brazil without Alisson Becker in goal were never entirely convincing, and Morocco's defensive organisation was exceptional throughout.

Morocco qualified from Group C as runners-up behind Brazil and face the Netherlands in the Round of 32. Don't write them off.

🇨🇦 3. Canada's Statement: 6-0 vs Qatar

Group B · June 18Canada 🇨🇦 6 – 0 🇶🇦 Qatar
HOST STORY

Not an upset in the traditional sense — Qatar were always going to struggle. But Canada's performance was a statement. Jonathan David hit a hat-trick. Alphonso Davies ran Qatar's defence ragged. A co-host nation, playing in front of their own fans for the first time at a World Cup, delivered a performance that announced their tournament ambitions to the world.

Canada qualified from Group B as runners-up behind Switzerland and face South Africa in the Round of 32 on June 28 — the opening day of the knockout stage.

🇺🇾 4. Uruguay Eliminated — Suárez's Farewell

Group HUruguay 🇺🇾 Eliminated
SHOCK EXIT

Uruguay were a dark horse going into the tournament. They left it without reaching the knockout stage. Spain and Cape Verde both finished above them in Group H, leaving Luis Suárez — almost certainly playing his final World Cup — without the farewell he deserved. A painful end for one of the most decorated strikers of his generation.

🇦🇺 5. Australia Shock Türkiye

Group D · June 13Australia 🇦🇺 2 – 0 🇹🇷 Türkiye
SURPRISE

Türkiye were one of Europe's form teams heading into the tournament. Australia's organised pressing game made it look easy. Two goals, a clean sheet, and Australia top of Group D after the first round. They eventually finished third and qualified as one of the eight best third-placed teams — facing Belgium in the Round of 32.

🏳️ 6. The Mother-Son World Cup Moment

Group G · June 15New Zealand 🇳🇿 vs Iran 🇮🇷 — A Historic Moment
HUMAN STORY

The score ended in a draw. But the story was something else entirely. When Tyler Bindon took the field for New Zealand against Iran, he became part of the first mother-son duo to each appear in a World Cup. His mother Jenny Bindon played for New Zealand's women's national team in 2007 and 2011. Football writing itself.
