7 Tipping Strategies That Actually Work

1
Always tip the clear favourite in Group Stage. France, Germany, England, Brazil, Argentina and Spain are all heavy favourites in their groups. Back them — being clever for the sake of it loses points.
2
Back low-scoring games in big matches. France vs Senegal, England vs Croatia, Argentina vs Austria — these elite clashes tend to be tight. Tip 1-0 or 1-1 and you'll hit exact scores more often than you think.
3
Back high-scoring games in mismatches. Germany vs Curaçao, France vs Iraq, England vs Panama — tip 3-0 or 4-0. Favourites put big numbers on weak opponents at World Cups.
4
Target one upset per matchday. Every World Cup has shocks. Japan beat Germany and Spain in 2022. Saudi Arabia beat Argentina. Pick one underdog per day — if it lands, you're miles ahead of the field.
5
Track injury news before you tip. Messi's fitness, Alisson's replacement, key defenders out — injuries change the entire dynamic. Check our injury tracker before submitting your tips.
6
The dead rubber is a trap. When both teams in a group's final match are already qualified or eliminated, motivation is low. Tip low-scoring draws or shock results — the big guns rest their starters.
7
In knockouts, back draws going to extra time. The knockout stage has historically produced a huge number of draws. Tip 1-1 or 0-0 in tight knockout matches — you get 1pt for the correct result even if it goes to penalties.

Upsets Worth Backing

Every World Cup has shocks. Here are the upsets most likely to happen based on squad quality, group dynamics and tournament history:

🇯🇵 Japan to beat Netherlands (Group F)

Japan beat Germany and Spain in 2022. Their pressing system is specifically designed to dismantle possession-based European teams. Netherlands are good but beatable. This could be the shock of the group stage.

🇲🇦 Morocco to beat Brazil (Group C)

Morocco are no longer a surprise — they're a genuinely dangerous team. Brazil without Alisson and Estêvão are weaker than expected. Morocco's defensive record from 2022 is extraordinary. A draw or Morocco win is very possible.

🇸🇳 Senegal to top Group I above France

France are favourites but Senegal have quality throughout — Mané, Gueye, Mendy. An opening win over France on June 15 would change everything for both teams. Senegal are dangerous.
