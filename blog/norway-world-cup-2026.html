Key Players

⭐ Erling Haaland — Striker

The best striker in the world. Set a UEFA qualifying record with 16 goals. If Norway go far, it's because Haaland is unstoppable. At 25, this is his peak World Cup.

🎯 Martin Ødegaard — Captain / Attacking Midfield

Arsenal's creative force. Controls the tempo and links midfield to attack. When Ødegaard plays well, Norway play well. Expect him to be Norway's key man in tight games.

💪 Alexander Sörloth — Striker

Powerful, dangerous at set pieces and a reliable backup to Haaland. Can start when needed and has scored big goals for club and country.

🛡️ Sander Berge — Defensive Midfield

The engine of Norway's midfield. Won his 100th cap during qualification. Screens the defence and starts attacks with intelligent passing.

🧱 Kristoffer Ajer — Centre-Back

Commanding, calm under pressure. Key to Norway keeping clean sheets against France and Senegal.

Can Norway Qualify?

To advance from Group I, Norway need to:

  • Beat Iraq (Jun 15) — must-win, Haaland vs weak defence
  • Beat or draw Senegal (Jun 28) — the pivotal match
  • Avoid a heavy defeat to France (Jun 22) — goal difference could matter

Haaland against Iraq's defence is a mismatch — a 2-0 or 3-0 win is very possible. The Senegal match on June 28 will decide Norway's fate.

Prediction: Norway finish 2nd in Group I and advance to the Round of 32. Haaland scores 3-4 goals in the group stage.

Norway's World Cup History

Norway's last World Cup appearance was France 1998 — where they famously beat Brazil 2-1 in the group stage (Tore André Flo scored). They've qualified for only 3 World Cups in history: 1938, 1994, and 1998.

This is Norway's first World Cup in 28 years. With Haaland, it could be their best ever campaign.
