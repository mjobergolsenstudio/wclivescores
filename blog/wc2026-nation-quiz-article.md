## The 48-Nation World Cup: The Biggest Ever

The 2026 tournament is the largest in FIFA World Cup history. For the first time, **48 nations** are competing across **104 matches** in 16 stadiums spanning three countries.

That means more teams, more drama, more upsets — and more possible nations to identify with. The expanded format introduces 12 groups of four, with the top two from each group and the eight best third-placed teams advancing to a new Round of 32.

Some first-time qualifiers join the party: **Cape Verde, Curaçao, Jordan, and Uzbekistan** will all experience their debut World Cup appearances in 2026.

Precise, disciplined, and relentlessly consistent. Germany has won 4 World Cups through sheer organisation and tactical excellence. You don’t do things halfway — you do them right.

Emotional, brilliant, and capable of the extraordinary when it matters most. Argentina, defending champions after their stunning 2022 triumph, represent that perfect blend of chaos and genius.

Elegant and deceptively dangerous. France combines individual brilliance with collective intelligence better than almost anyone. You’re always five moves ahead.

### 🇪🇸 Spain — The Artist

Patient, technical, and committed to the beautiful game. Spain’s philosophy of possession-based football represents a worldview — that control, not chaos, is the path to greatness.
