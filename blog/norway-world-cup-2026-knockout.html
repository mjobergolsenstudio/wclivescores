Haaland: Four Goals, Endless Records

Two goals against Iraq. Two more against Senegal. Four goals in two games — and then Ståle Solbakken rested him completely for France. Haaland is already Norway's all-time leading World Cup scorer after just two appearances.

His two goals against Senegal were characteristic. The first, three minutes after half-time, was pure predatory instinct — Ødegaard slid the ball through, Haaland buried it. The second was even better: a stunning volley off the crossbar from Patrick Berg's cross that sent the Norwegian end wild. Four goals. Two games. Job done.

The fact he was rested for France — and Norway still nearly competed before conceding three to Dembélé — shows the squad depth that makes this team genuinely dangerous in the knockout rounds.

The Rowing Celebration That Went Viral

After every Norway goal, the same thing happens in the stands. Thousands of red-and-white clad supporters grab imaginary oars and row together, a traditional Norwegian celebration that has become one of the tournament's defining images. After Haaland's second against Senegal, the rowing lasted several minutes. Videos went around the world. For 28 years, Norwegian fans have waited. Now they're rowing their way through a World Cup.

The Knockout Stage: Ivory Coast on June 30

Round of 32
🇨🇮 Ivory Coast vs Norway 🇳🇴
June 30 · AT&T Stadium, Dallas · 19:00 local

Norway finish second in Group I behind France and face Ivory Coast — who qualified from Group E — in Dallas. Ivory Coast have Amad Diallo, Sébastien Haller and Simon Adingra. They're dangerous on the counter. But Norway with a rested Haaland and Martin Ødegaard in form are favourites to advance.

If Norway win, the path gets harder. Brazil or Japan await in the Round of 16. But this is a Norwegian team that went unbeaten through qualifying with a 100% win record. They believe. The fans believe. And with Haaland ready to play, so should you.
